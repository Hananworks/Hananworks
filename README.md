# 💫 About Me:
We are rapidly heading toward a world entirely driven by AI.A world where absolute privacy is becoming an illusion, data exploitation is effortless, and the digital landscape is bound to get incredibly messy. I didnot want to just watch this transformation happen.I wanted to understand the mechanics behind securing it. That is what sparked my obsession with cybersecurity. As a Computer Science student at National Textile University, my journey into tech began with a simple, relentless question: "What happens if we break this rule instead of following that one?" This curiosity naturally pulled me away from the surface layer of software and dragged me deep into the low-level architecture of how systems actually operate, defend, and fail.

I thrive on understanding the inner workings of an operating system and network topology. I don't just use tools; I focus heavily on OS internals and fundamental security mechanics. My technical foundation includes an in-depth understanding of Ring Privileges, User vs. Kernel mode transitions, System Calls, the OS Boot Process, advanced Linux commands, and robust Computer Networking. Beyond low-level systems, I am skilled in software development using C# and Object-Oriented Programming (OOP). I also bring a unique blend of creativity and leadership to the table, serving as a Graphic Member for the campus Sustainable Development Goals (SDG) society using Canva, and actively participating as an Esports member in the Campus Society of Computer Science.

My ultimate mission is clear: to engineer software and architect defenses that protect critical data from evolving cyber threats. I position myself as a first-principles problem solver who doesn't just patch vulnerabilities but seeks to understand the root cause of systemic risks. Whether it is decoding a complex system call, optimizing network defenses, or designing secure applications for an AI-driven future, I am driven by the challenge of turning digital chaos into structured, bulletproof security.
